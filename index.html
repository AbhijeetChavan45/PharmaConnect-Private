How it works

Three Simple Steps to Your Medicine

No more wandering pharmacy to pharmacy. PharmaConnect gets you what you need, fast.

Search Your Medicine

Type the medicine name or scan your prescription. Our database covers 10,000+ medicines with real-time stock data.

Discover Nearby Pharmacies

See pharmacies on a live map sorted by distance, availability, and price. Compare all options at a glance.

Reserve or Get Delivery

Book your medicine for pickup or request home delivery. Track your order in real time until it arrives.
